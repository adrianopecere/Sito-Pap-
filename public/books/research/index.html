Crisi e storia. A partire da Ernesto de Martino

The work of Ernesto de Martino is now at the centre of an international rediscovery, proving fertile ground not only for the anthropology of the third millennium but also for psychiatry, philosophy, history and literature. Crisi e storia explores this interdisciplinary vocation, bringing together the voices of scholars who have given decisive new turns to its interpretation and relaunched de Martino’s thought from fresh perspectives. Among the themes addressed in the volume are the relevance today of his view of the Italian South – from the old “border at Eboli” to the new Afro-European frontier of “caporalato”, the exploitation of migrant farm labour –, the Balkan folklore of vampires and werewolves, his dialogue with Cassirer and Heidegger, the political dimension of magical thinking, and Basaglia’s psychiatry as a space of decolonial practices. Edited by Stefano De Matteis and Paolo Pecere – among the most authoritative contemporary interpreters of Ernesto de Martino – the book shows how the thought of the greatest Italian anthropologist of the twentieth century remains an indispensable tool for understanding the individual and collective crises of our time.

La natura della mente. Da Cartesio alle scienze cognitive

What is the mind? How does it relate to the body? What are the foundations of our cognitive capacities and of personal identity? This volume offers a critical history of research on these questions from the seventeenth century to the present, highlighting the continuities and discontinuities between contemporary studies and their modern antecedents. It also provides critical, up-to-date accounts of the works of classic authors such as Descartes, Spinoza, Locke, Kant and Freud, and examines the philosophical foundations of the models of cognitive neuroscience, with particular attention to issues such as the metaphysical interpretation of scientific data, materialist reductionism and the animal mind.

Mechanism, Life and Mind in Modern Natural Philosophy

This volume emphasizes the diversity and fruitfulness of early modern mechanism as a program, as a concept, as a model. Mechanistic study of the living body but also of the mind and mental processes are examined in careful historical focus, dealing with figures ranging from the first-rank (Bacon, Descartes, Spinoza, Cudworth, Gassendi, Locke, Leibniz, Kant) to less well-known individuals (Scaliger, Martini) or prominent natural philosophers who have been neglected in recent years (Willis, Steno, etc.). The volume moves from early modern medicine and physiology to late Enlightenment and even early 19th-century psychology.

Soul, Mind and Brain from Descartes to Cognitive Science

This interdisciplinary book ties the historical work of Descartes to his successors through current research and critical overviews on the neuroscience of consciousness, the brain, and cognition. This text is the first historical survey to focus on the cohesions and discontinuities between historical and contemporary thinkers working in philosophy, physiology, psychology, and neuroscience.

Il libro della natura, vol. I

Philosophy and the modern sciences are historically rooted in the same field of inquiry and often continue to address common questions. The book brings out this kinship by examining fundamental theories and problems of modern science.

Il libro della natura, vol. II

Philosophy and the modern sciences grew out of the same field of inquiry and continue to address common questions, yet today they are often regarded as two entirely different forms of knowledge. The volume brings out their kinship by examining the problems and theories that most stimulated philosophical reflection on the sciences in the twentieth century.

Dalla parte di Alice

This book addresses a central theme of modern philosophical and literary culture, the interweaving of personal identity and narrative imagination, and identifies a crucial ambivalence in it: the imaginary can serve as a way of reworking reality and individual life, but it can also become a place where reality is concealed and alienation takes hold.

Meccanica quantistica, rappresentazione, realtà

Quantum mechanics is one of the most powerful and successful theories of contemporary science, yet from the outset it has been accompanied by intense debate about its interpretation and its ultimate validity. The authors describe the unresolved issues in the physical and mathematical formulation of the theory and propose a rethinking of its various interpretations.
