Vivere la conoscenza

Clear and concise, and rich in concrete examples, the textbook is easy to study independently and is complemented by a wide selection of well-annotated texts. Its interdisciplinary approach, the hallmark of the work, also extends to civic education (with particular attention to the themes of East and West, environment and ecology, and women's thought).

Filosofia in dialogo

A concise yet complete philosophy course that looks to current affairs and interdisciplinarity to engage students and develop their critical thinking.

Publisher’s page

La ricerca della conoscenza

A philosophy textbook in six volumes. The course proposes a new approach to the study of philosophy, which is connected with other disciplines and becomes the basis for developing transferable skills and forming aware, responsible and active citizens: numerous interdisciplinary sections link philosophy to history, literature, art and science, to make the most of the subject's educational potential.

Publisher’s page

Le vie della conoscenza

A philosophy course in three volumes, full of prompts for a formative approach to the subject: the clear, linear style of exposition guides students through the analysis not only of philosophical theories but also of the historical and cultural contexts in which they were produced. The text includes interdisciplinary sections (linking philosophy to history, literature and science), analyses of works of art and profiles devoted to women's thought.

Publisher’s page

Fisica per filosofi

For the first time, the book offers students, teachers and interested readers a formally simplified exposition of the principles of physics which, accompanied by historical and philosophical insights, ranges from classical mechanics to today's frontier theories by way of thermodynamics, the theories of relativity and quantum mechanics.
