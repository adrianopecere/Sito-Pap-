Privacy

This website is a personal site that presents the work of Paolo Pecere. It does not use cookies or advertising, and it has no forms or user accounts.

To know which pages are read, the site counts visits with GoatCounter, a statistics service that does not use cookies and does not store visitors’ IP addresses. For each visit it records the page, the referring site, the type of browser and operating system, the screen size and the country (derived from the IP address, which is not kept). The site owner sees only aggregate figures and cannot identify individual visitors.

Like any website, it is delivered by a hosting provider whose servers may record technical data needed to serve the pages (such as IP address, date and time, and the page requested) in their logs. The site owner does not use these data to identify visitors.

The fonts and images are served from this site itself; only the small visit-counting script is loaded from GoatCounter. Links to publishers, journals, video and podcast platforms open external sites, each with its own privacy policy.

For any question about this page you can write to the address in the contacts.
