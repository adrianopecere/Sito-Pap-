About

Paolo Pecere is a historian of philosophy and a writer of fiction and literary non-fiction.

He is associate professor at the University of Roma Tre.

His last scholarly monograph is Soul, Mind and Brain from Descartes to Cognitive Science (Springer 2020; expanded edition: La natura della mente, Carocci 2023).

His first novel La vita lontana was published in 2018 and he is the author of Risorgere (2019), Il dio che danza (2021) and Il senso della natura (2024).
